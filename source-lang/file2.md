# Getting started

Welcome to the Northwind field guide. This short page explains how a new teammate sets up a workspace and invites a colleague.

## Create your workspace

1. Open the app and choose **Create workspace**.
2. Enter a workspace name, such as Northwind Studio.
3. Pick the language you write in. You can add translation languages later.

Your workspace is ready when you see the home screen.

## Invite a teammate

Send an invite from the People page. Your colleague receives an email with a link that expires after seven days. After they join, they can read this guide in their own language. asdf

If the invite expires, send a new one. You do not need to create the workspace again.
